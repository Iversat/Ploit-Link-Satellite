The JPEG is distributed unchanged. At runtime PLS projects the panorama onto
the observer's celestial sphere, scales its display brightness below the horizon,
and blends it over the camera in AR. This is a historic photographic background,
not a live view; bright Solar System objects visible in the source are not
calculated current planet positions. No endorsement by ESO is implied.

This image retains its CC BY 4.0 license and is excluded from the project's
custom source license and project-name restrictions. The credit is displayed
in the night-sky controls and linked to the original image.
